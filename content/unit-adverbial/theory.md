# Mệnh Đề Trạng Ngữ (Adverbial Clauses)

Bổ sung ý nghĩa cho **động từ** hoặc **cả câu**.

## Thời gian

when, while, before, after, until, as soon as

- Call me **when** you arrive.

## Lý do

because, as, since

- He stayed home **because** he was ill.

## Mục đích

to-infinitive, in order to, so that

- She studied hard **so that** she could pass.

## Tương phản / nhượng bộ

although, though, even though, whereas, while

- **Although** it rained, we went out.

## Kết quả

so… that, such… that

- It was **so cold that** we stayed inside.
