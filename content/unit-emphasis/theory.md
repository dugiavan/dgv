# Nhấn Mạnh & Đảo Ngữ (Emphasis & Inversion)

## Câu chẻ (Cleft sentences)

- **It is/was … that**: It was **Tim** who ran into the office.
- **What … is/was**: What I need **is** more time.

## Đảo ngữ (Inversion)

Đưa trợ động từ lên trước chủ ngữ:

- **Never/Seldom/Hardly** + aux + S + V…
  - Never **have I seen** such a view.
- **Câu điều kiện bỏ If**:
  - Had I known, I would have helped.
  - Were I you, I would agree.
